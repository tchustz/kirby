## September 6, Quality form review

**Source: Elias Ward, Director of Care Quality, working notes**

The standard review form asks whether the Guide followed process, communicated clearly, used CLEAR, and closed the call. It works reasonably for broad coaching trends but does not capture a product-specific decision that has not yet been published. Elias wrote that “followed process” can conceal two very different conversations: a Guide may complete a generic restart flow accurately, or a Guide may recognize that repeated restarts are no longer the appropriate action.

He also noted a measurement limit. Quality reviews a sample of calls and post-call feedback comes from customers who choose to respond. Neither can establish the full rate of behavior in the queue. The purpose of a temporary focused sample would be to see patterns and improve the response, not to create a precise performance ranking.

**Source: Quality reviewer Sienna Patel, comment in the form review**

“We sometimes code empathy high when a Guide has stayed warm for 20 minutes. That can hide that the customer still has no answer. For this issue, I want to listen for a concrete acknowledgment and a useful pivot. I do not need a perfect sentence.”

**Source: Quality reviewer Malik, comment**

“We should not make the new fields a compliance trap. A Guide with an ineligible unit may do normal troubleshooting well. We need the sample notes to say what pattern was present and what the serial result was.”

## September 18, manager practice experiment
