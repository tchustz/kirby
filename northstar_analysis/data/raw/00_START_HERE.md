# Customer Care work sample

This fictional work sample gives us concrete work to discuss in your final interview. Northstar Home, its people, products, customers, and records are synthetic. We will not use what you create for a real business purpose.

You are a learning experience designer supporting Customer Care at Northstar Home, a large consumer home-device company. Treat October 2, 2026 as today in the case. Start with `2026-09-29-dana-email.md`, the request from Dana, the Director of Customer Care. A teammate has gathered the other material in `sources/`. Your upcoming meeting with Dana is to review your recommendation before she updates supervisors. She has asked for the materials you want her to read before that meeting.

## Your assignment

Prepare a draft email you would send Dana ahead of that meeting, and create the materials you would attach to it. Use the email to orient her to the materials, your recommended direction, and where she should look first. Decide what materials are appropriate. At least one should contain actual words a learner would see or hear. Finished visual design is not expected.

Use Codex, Claude Code, or a comparable coding-agent harness. You may use local scripts and tools. Use only the fictional information in this packet for case facts. If you need to assume something, label the assumption. Do not contact real people or companies for this exercise.

## Time and deliverables

Your recruiter's email sets the work window and deadline. The calendar window lets you schedule the work; it is not an expectation that you work continuously until the deadline. Submit the most useful work you can, even if it is partial. Elapsed time and the amount of polish are not the point of the exercise.

If you need an accommodation for this work sample or the interview, contact your recruiter as soon as you know, including if the need arises after you receive the materials. We will work with you to identify an appropriate arrangement. Keep the request separate from your work submission. If access to a coding-agent harness is a barrier, contact your recruiter; their email explains the available access support.

Follow the submission method in your recruiter's email. Your work should have two clearly labeled parts:

1. Dana's pre-read: one standalone file containing your draft email to Dana, plus one or more additional files containing the materials named in that email. Do not send anything to Dana or anyone at Northstar Home.
2. A brief note for your interviewers, separate from Dana's materials. Summarize how you understood the challenge, why you chose your approach, how you worked with your coding-agent harness, which sources mattered, what you checked or corrected, and what remains uncertain. Include enough concrete examples for us to discuss your process with you. You may use `INTERVIEW_PREP_PROMPT.md` to help draft this note, or use your own method.

You do not need to resubmit the supplied sources. Include any working files needed to understand your claims or the materials you created. File format and naming are up to you, but make it clear which materials are intended for Dana and which note is for the interviewers.

We will read your submission before the final interview. The conversation will explore your judgment, design choices, source use, checks, and collaboration with your harness. There is no single prescribed solution or required asset format.
