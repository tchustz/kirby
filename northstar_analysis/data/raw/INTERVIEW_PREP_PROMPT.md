# Optional interview prep prompt

You may give the text below to your coding agent near the end of the exercise. You may prepare the brief note for your interviewers another way.

> Help me draft a concise note for the interviewers who will discuss this work sample with me. Use only task-local files and the interaction history you can actually access. Keep this note separate from the email and materials for Dana. Identify the materials I created, how I framed the challenge and any important change in my thinking, why I chose this approach, an alternative I considered, and the sources behind my most important claims. Describe how I directed and used the coding-agent harness, with a few concrete examples of my instructions, tools, scripts, or checks. Include errors or weak claims I found and corrected, plus uncertainty and useful next questions. Distinguish my decisions from your suggestions or output. Do not invent actions, prompts, checks, or decisions. Mark anything you cannot verify as `Candidate to confirm`.

Read and correct the note yourself before submission. You may omit unrelated personal or employer information from agent history.
